About WECOW LTD

WECOW LTD (Women Empowerment and Community Wellness) is a Kenyan organization dedicated to empowering women and communities through financial literacy, table banking, entrepreneurship development, and economic empowerment.

Vision

To build financially empowered, self-reliant, and prosperous communities.

Mission

To empower women and communities through financial literacy, entrepreneurship, leadership development, and sustainable economic opportunities.

The WECOW Journey

Seven transformative steps empowering women to save together, grow businesses, improve families, and strengthen communities.

Step 1
Women Meet

Women from the same community come together with a shared vision of financial independence and mutual support. They form organized groups ready to embark on a journey of transformation.

Step 2
Table Banking

Groups establish table banking structures where members make regular savings contributions, access emergency loans, and manage group finances collectively. This creates a safety net and lending mechanism.

Step 3
Financial Training

Members receive hands-on training in financial literacy including budgeting, saving strategies, investment basics, bookkeeping, and responsible money management skills.

Step 4
Business Start-up

Armed with skills and savings, women launch income-generating enterprises. WECOW provides business planning support, market research guidance, and mentorship to ensure sustainable business start-ups.

Step 5
Business Growth

With ongoing mentorship and support, businesses expand their reach, increase profits, and create employment opportunities. Women develop leadership capabilities and reinvest in their enterprises.

Step 6
Family Improvement

Increased household income directly improves family welfare—better nutrition, improved housing, quality education for children, and access to healthcare services strengthen family foundations.

Step 7
Community Development

Strong families build strong communities. Empowered women become community leaders, advocate for social change, establish welfare programs, and create sustainable development initiatives benefiting all.

Services
